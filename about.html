ABOUT THE CREATOR

Behind The Pressure to Keep Up

A personal reflection turned into an interactive experience about social media, comparison, and learning to grow at your own pace.

CREATOR & STUDENT

Hi, I'm Anne Aros.

BSIT — Marketing Technology

I created The Pressure to Keep Up as a reflective web experience exploring how social media comparison can influence the way we see ourselves, our progress, and our lives.

The idea was inspired by my own experiences with comparing myself to what I saw online. Over time, I became more aware that constantly measuring my life against other people's achievements, appearances, and carefully presented moments could affect how I viewed myself.

This project turns that realization into something other young adults can interact with. It is not meant to tell people to leave social media. Instead, it encourages us to notice our habits, reflect on what genuinely matters to us, and choose healthier boundaries that fit our own lives.

01

WHY I CREATED THIS

Growth doesn't have to look the same for everyone.

One of the most important things I learned from my experience is that another person's timeline does not determine the value of my own. Self-acceptance does not mean refusing to improve. It means being able to grow because something matters to me, rather than because I feel pressured to become someone else.

I hope this website gives visitors a quiet moment to think about their own relationship with social media and remember that progress can happen at different times, in different ways, and for different reasons.
