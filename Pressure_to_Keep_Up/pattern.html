03 · THE PATTERN

Comparison can become a cycle.

A single post may seem harmless. But repeated comparison can influence how we judge ourselves, present ourselves online, and seek validation.

THE IMPORTANT PART

The problem isn't simply seeing other people.

The pressure grows when someone else's carefully presented life becomes the standard we repeatedly use to measure our own worth, progress, appearance, or success.
