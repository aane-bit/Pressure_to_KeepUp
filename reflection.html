Bring the attention back to you.

Social media gives us many opportunities to look outward. This part asks you to temporarily stop measuring yourself against what is happening on a screen.

ONLINE SELF

Am I sharing this because it genuinely makes me happy, or because I want people to see me a certain way?

YOUR TIMELINE

Am I using another person's progress as the measurement of my own?

Think about achievements, education, careers, relationships, money, travel, appearance, or any milestone that makes you feel as if you should already be somewhere else.

YOUR VALUES

What actually matters to me when nobody online is watching?

Think beyond likes, trends, appearances, and other people's timelines. What parts of your life still matter when there is no audience?
