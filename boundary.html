05 · BOUNDARY

Choose what protects your attention.

A healthy boundary does not have to mean leaving social media completely. The goal is to choose one realistic change that gives you more control over how you use it.

✦

Choose something realistic enough that you could actually try it.

YOUR ACTION PLAN

Step 1 of 3

CHOOSE ONE

Which boundary feels most useful for you right now?

You are not choosing a permanent rule. Think of this as something you can experiment with for the next seven days.

FIND YOUR REASON

What would you like to give more attention to instead?

A boundary is easier to understand when it protects something that personally matters to you.

✦

YOUR 7-DAY COMMITMENT

Make the intention simple and specific.

FOR THE NEXT 7 DAYS

I will choose a healthier boundary because I want to focus more on what matters to me .

Remember

The goal is not perfection. At the end of seven days, notice what helped, what was difficult, and what you would change.
